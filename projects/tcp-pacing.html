Hardware-offloaded TCP pacing

Lead Researcher · GNA-G Data Intensive Science Working Group

  • Cutting packet loss on 100 Gbps transoceanic science links by pacing TCP in NVIDIA BlueField DPU hardware.
  • Ran a baseline on a real 100G Los Angeles – Amsterdam path (165 ms RTT). Designing the comparison of no pacing, Linux software pacing and NIC hardware pacing.
  • Designing an adaptive controller on the DPU's Arm cores that sets the ConnectX hardware rate limiter per flow.
