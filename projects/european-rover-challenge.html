European Rover Challenge

Mechatronics Team Member

  • Mechatronics Team Member, September 2025 – present: designing the robotic arm and chassis subsystems and integrating power electronics for sensors and wheel actuation.
  • Separately, Team Leader of KCL RedForge for 2026/27, the undergrad engineering team that is now the home of this project.

These are two current roles, not one. As a mechatronics member I work on the arm, the chassis, and the power electronics that drive sensors and the wheels. As Team Leader of RedForge I lead the wider team for the 2026/27 season.

Last year KCL Electronics entered the European Rover Challenge, a space-robotics competition run by the European Space Foundation, and began building a 75 kg Mars rover (RF-01 in the team's later CAD). The team got about 90% of the way and missed qualifying on a drone power fault. This year RedForge is an application-based team whose flagship is still ERC — a 75 kg rover with a drone and a robotic arm, tested on science, navigation, repair and autonomous drone tasks on an outdoor Mars Yard — and it is also running MachX, a UK rocketry competition.
