Leakage-aware surface-code decoder

Independent research · heralded erasure on a rotated surface code

  • At d = 7 and heralded-erasure rate 2×10⁻², adaptive PyMatching's logical error rate per round is 16.0× lower than static matching (1.70×10⁻³ vs 1.06×10⁻⁴, 600,000 shots).
  • Extrapolated qubit savings from fits at d = 3, 5 and 7 reach 4.78× at that rate, and 1.23× when the erasure rate equals the Pauli rate (2×10⁻³), for a 10⁻⁶ window target.
  • Heralded erasure is a simplified stand-in for leakage, not leakage itself.
